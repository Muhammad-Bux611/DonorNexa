About The Platform

Connecting Donors. Bridging Lives. Saving Hope.

DonorNexa is a smart, enterprise-grade emergency blood-donor coordination platform designed to eliminate fatal delays during critical transfusions across Pakistan.

🏥

Verified Hospitals

Every healthcare facility undergoes strict administrative license verification with binary header validation before issuing emergency blood requests.

🩸

Clinical Eligibility

Automated clinical screening guarantees donor safety by strictly checking Age (18–60), Weight (≥50 kg), Active Availability, and a 90-Day Cooldown.

⚡

Intelligent Ranking

Red blood cell compatibility mapping combines with geographic proximity, cooldown interval freshness, and urgency to rank the most optimal candidates.

How DonorNexa Works

1
Emergency Issued

A verified hospital creates an urgent blood request with required units and blood group.

2
Candidate Ranking

DonorNexa scores eligible voluntary donors based on proximity and ABO/Rh compatibility.

3
Secure Coordination

Matched donors receive instant alerts. Two-way communication coordinates arrival time.

4
Donation & Badges

Upon confirmation, the hospital inventory updates, and donors earn milestone badges.
